Privacy Policy

Learn how StudyNest handles and protects your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide a personalized academic tracking experience. This includes:

  • Name and email address used for the student account.
  • Account authentication information.
  • Academic tasks and their deadlines.
  • Study schedules and session details.
  • Task statuses and progress information.
  • Feedback submitted through the website.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is used strictly to operate and improve StudyNest. This includes:

  • Creating and managing student accounts.
  • Saving academic tasks.
  • Organizing study sessions.
  • Displaying task summaries and progress.
  • Responding to feedback and technical concerns.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your information is stored securely using Supabase, a third-party backend service. We implement row-level security policies to ensure that only you can access and modify your own tasks and study sessions. We do not claim to use end-to-end encryption for all data, but we rely on Supabase's industry-standard security infrastructure.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your data is processed by Supabase (our hosting and database provider) solely to provide the service. We do not share your academic tasks with other students.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account remains active. If you wish to request the deletion of your account and all associated academic data, please contact our support team.

6. Contact Information

For questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled, please contact us via the Contact & Feedback page.
