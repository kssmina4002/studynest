Please review the guidelines for using StudyNest.

StudyNest is a student study-planning and task-tracking system designed to help users organize academic activities, plan study sessions, and monitor their progress.

By creating an account, you agree to:

You are responsible for entering accurate task details, deadlines, and study schedules. StudyNest is a tool to help you organize your work; it does not guarantee academic results, grades, or automatically ensure that deadlines are met.

If you wish to close your account and have your data deleted, please contact us via the Contact & Feedback page. We will process deletion requests in accordance with our data retention policies.
